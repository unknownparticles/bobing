Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: desktop web game interface concept, a single finished screen.
Primary request: Refine the existing Chinese Mid-Autumn dice strategy game《今晚谁是状元》in a refined light contemporary Chinese folk art style. This is an implementable UI art direction reference, with readable controls and clearly separated gameplay information.

Style: warm ivory rice paper, deep ink green type, restrained cinnabar red primary controls, celadon ceramic, fine brass details and delicate original Minnan cloud patterns. Elegant illustrated indie tabletop game, tactile but restrained, beautiful editorial typography with Chinese serif headings and clean readable UI labels. Soft natural light, subtle paper fibers, richer contrast than a faded beige website. No photorealistic environment.

Composition: a single flat, front-facing landscape game screenshot, no device or browser chrome. Entire screen fits with generous safe margins. Slim top header with a red square 博 seal, game title, round indicator "第 03 / 08 轮" and sound control. Five compact illustrated contemporary office coworker portraits form an orderly row across the upper main play area, each with a short Chinese name and large score. Highlight one active player clearly. Do not invent ancient-costume heroes: these are contemporary coworkers at an office Mid-Autumn dinner.

Center-left is the largest focal area: a beautiful shallow celadon dice bowl, viewed slightly from above, containing exactly six ivory six-sided dice with crisp black pips and red four pips. Keep dice large and legible; every die rests inside the bowl. A subtle circular tabletop ornament and small restrained cloud flourishes frame the bowl, leaving substantial clean space. Above the bowl, a clear compact turn pill "轮到你了" and a secondary small field label "标准团圆席 · 6 颗骰子". Below the bowl, a clear result heading and one short line of contextual guidance. Decorative art must not obscure the results or controls.

Right side about 22% width: a neatly structured light paper prize panel titled "奖池一览". Prominent remaining points, then exactly six orderly prize rows named "状元", "对堂", "三红", "四进", "二举", "一秀" with clear small stock counts and restrained distinct icons. This is supporting information; it must not compete with the dice bowl.

Bottom: one fully visible action dock with the current player's portrait, name "林小满", score and "48 金币" on the left. To the right, three clearly separated secondary controls "角色技能", "重掷券", "我的手牌" with small explanatory captions. The dominant wide cinnabar button says "掷骰，博个好彩头". Tiny optional phone shake control at the far end. Strong visual distinction between primary and secondary actions; all text, buttons and the entire bottom dock are inside the screen, not clipped.

Materials: celadon bowl has a believable glazed rim and restrained highlights, dice have soft readable dimensional shading, paper panels use subtle physical edges. Decorative motifs remain limited to the outer edges and unused space. Keep panels and text surfaces plain enough to rebuild with HTML and CSS.
Constraints: retain the light paper/celadon visual identity, exactly five opponent portraits plus one current player, exactly six dice, one prize panel, one bottom dock. This is one coherent usable screen, not a poster. Chinese titles and primary actions should be accurate. Short secondary prose may be represented by clean typesetting. No watermark, no mockup frame, no collage, no giant decorative calligraphy, no dense unreadable paragraphs, no modern admin dashboard, no neon, no fantasy RPG HUD, no additional mechanics.
